Program Name

Bard College RKC Monitor SMS Alerts

Program Description

Bard College RKC Monitor SMS Alerts provide automated operational alerts for laboratory freezer and refrigerator monitoring.

Message Types

Messages may include temperature alarms, door-open alarms, power interruptions, communication failures, escalation notices, resolution notices, and test messages.

Message Frequency and Costs

  • Message frequency varies based on monitored equipment events.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

User Commands

  • Reply YES to acknowledge an alert when requested.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.

Additional Terms

  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Participation is limited to authorized Bard College personnel.
