BardBox Monitoring Platform Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026

BardBox is a Bard College monitoring platform for laboratory, research, and environmental systems. The RKC Monitor sends operational SMS alerts for laboratory freezer and refrigerator monitoring.

Information Collected

Data collected may include recipient name, role or department, mobile number, alert preferences, acknowledgment responses such as YES, opt-out status, and message delivery records.

How Information Is Used

Data is used only to send operational monitoring alerts and manage the alert escalation workflow.

SMS Consent and Mobile Numbers

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in or consent data are not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or shared with affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Program Details

  • Message frequency varies based on system conditions.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Users may reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

Support

For questions about this privacy policy or BardBox SMS alerts, contact bardbox@bard.edu.
